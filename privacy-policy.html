1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated by your use of our services, and information from third-party sources.

Account & Registration Data: Name, email address, company name, password (hashed), and billing information when you create an account on any Rip It Labs platform (RILBOT, Strut, SoulCoach, YTax, or others).

Usage Data: Agent queries and responses, session duration, features accessed, device type, browser, operating system, and IP address.

Payment Data: Credit card transactions are processed by Stripe and never stored on our servers. RILCOIN (cryptocurrency) wallet addresses and transaction hashes are recorded for payment verification.

Communications: Emails, support tickets, and feedback you send us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and improve our AI agent platforms and applications
  • Process payments and generate billing records
  • Send transactional emails (invoices, password resets, verification links)
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Generate aggregate, anonymized analytics about platform usage

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data to train AI models without explicit consent.

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Stripe (payments), Google Cloud (infrastructure), Cloudflare (CDN/security), and email delivery providers — each under appropriate data processing agreements
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Rip It Labs, our users, or the public
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — you will be notified in advance
  • With Your Consent: In any other case, only with your explicit permission

4. Blockchain & On-Chain Data

⚠️ Blockchain data is public and permanent. Transaction hashes, wallet addresses, and proof-of-execution receipts written to the Base or Polygon blockchain are immutable and visible to anyone.

When you pay with RILCOIN or use blockchain-verified features, your wallet address and transaction details are recorded on a public blockchain. This data cannot be deleted or modified. Do not use a wallet address you wish to keep private for RILBOT transactions.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use the following types of cookies and local storage:

  • Essential: Authentication tokens, session identifiers — required for the service to function
  • Functional: Language preferences, UI preferences, onboarding state
  • Analytics: Aggregate usage statistics — anonymized, no cross-site tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or sell cookie data to ad networks. You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it (e.g., financial records, which are retained for 7 years).

Blockchain transaction records cannot be deleted due to the immutable nature of the blockchain.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 10)
  • JWT tokens with expiration and denylist for revocation
  • TLS encryption in transit via Cloudflare
  • Rate limiting on all authentication endpoints
  • Audit logging of all sensitive actions
  • Regular security reviews and penetration testing

No system is completely secure. In the event of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you within 72 hours as required by applicable law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@ripitlabs.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or by posting a notice on our platform at least 30 days before the change takes effect. Your continued use of our services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
