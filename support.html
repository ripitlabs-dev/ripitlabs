FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
On the login page of any Rip It Labs platform, click "Forgot password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link valid for 1 hour. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within 5 minutes.
What is RILCOIN and how do I use it?
RILCOIN is a utility token used to pay for AI agent tasks on RILBOT. Paying with RILCOIN gives you a 20% discount vs. credit card. Connect a Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, or WalletConnect) on rilbot.ripitlabs.com to get started.
How do I request a refund?
Refunds are available within 7 days of purchase if you've made fewer than 5 API requests. Email support@ripitlabs.com with your account email and the charge date. Cryptocurrency payments are generally non-refundable due to the irreversible nature of blockchain transactions.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Log in to your account, go to Settings → Billing, and click "Cancel Subscription." Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. You can also email support@ripitlabs.com and we'll cancel for you.
Is my data private? Do you train AI on my data?
Your data is never used to train AI models without your explicit consent. Each workspace is fully isolated — other customers cannot access your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
What AI model powers the agents?
RILBOT agents are powered by Anthropic's Claude models. We use the latest available model (currently Claude Sonnet 4.6) with domain-specific system prompts that give each agent its specialization.
How do I get RILBOT on Google Cloud?
Visit rilbot.ripitlabs.com/marketplace and click "Get Started on Google Cloud." You'll need an existing Google Cloud account. Billing is handled through your GCP invoice.
I found a security vulnerability. How do I report it?
Email security@ripitlabs.com with a description of the vulnerability. We take security reports seriously and will respond within 48 hours. Please do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities before we've had a chance to address them.
How do I delete my account and data?
Email privacy@ripitlabs.com with your account email and the subject "Account Deletion Request." We'll delete your account and personal data within 30 days, as required by our Privacy Policy.
Do you offer API access?
Yes. Business and Enterprise plans include full REST API access. API documentation is available after login. Contact tim@ripitlabs.com for enterprise API arrangements.
