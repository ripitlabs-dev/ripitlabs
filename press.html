Full bio

Tim Cavin is the founder, CEO, CTO and COO of Rip It Labs, an Arizona technology company that builds AI agents and the proof infrastructure that holds them accountable.

Cavin served in the U.S. Army from 1997 to 2005. He then spent 20 years as an investigator, including Special Investigations Unit (SIU) work with Arizona Investigative Services and other investigation firms. That career is the thread through everything he builds: evidence, verification, and a record that cannot be quietly changed.

He has been involved in crypto since the OpenCoin and NewCoin era of 2012, the company that became Ripple, building and contributing to open-source projects wherever he could. He previously served as President of Cavin Research Group, a research and development firm.

In February 2026 he founded Rip It Labs, which he calls the greatest project of his life. He created its products, including RILBOT, an AI agent marketplace where every run is signed and anchored on-chain; RIL Warden, a jail and flight recorder for AI agents; RILchain and RILoracle; VoteChain; ProofRail; CyberStrike; and the AEGIS and SHIELD defense tools. The company's products anchor their records on five public blockchains: XDC, Base, Hedera, the XRP Ledger and Stellar.

Story angles

  • From investigator to proof engineer. Twenty years of investigations taught Cavin that evidence only matters if nobody can alter it. Rip It Labs turns that into software: signed, on-chain records for AI agent work, documents, votes and oracle data.
  • A jail for AI agents. RIL Warden limits AI agents to approved sites, keeps keys out of their hands, plants decoy credentials and records everything in a signed, tamper-evident log. Built and launched in September 2026.
  • Army veteran building defense tools. AEGIS and SHIELD are the company's defense tools. SHIELD is a defensive, receive-only signal-integrity tool.
  • Crypto since 2012, chain-neutral in 2026. Cavin has been in crypto since the OpenCoin era. His company anchors records on five chains rather than betting on one.
  • A company built at AI speed. Rip It Labs went from its first commit in March 2026 to more than 20 live products by fall 2026. Its dated timeline is public and anchored on-chain.
