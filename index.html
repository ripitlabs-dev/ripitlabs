How we build

Built to be checked, not just trusted.

The design rules behind every Rip It Labs platform.

POEProof of execution

Signed and anchored

Agent actions and key records are signed with Ed25519 and their fingerprints anchored on public blockchains. Anyone can verify a receipt without access to our systems.

ISOFinancial data

ISO 20022-aligned

Our finance and trade apps use structured, ISO 20022-aligned data models for payments, trade finance and settlement workflows.

SECSecurity

Least privilege by default

Keys kept out of agents' reach, approved destinations only, end-to-end encryption where messages must stay private, and tamper-evident logs of what happened.

RILOwnership

Built from scratch

From the FastAPI backends to the React frontends to the smart contracts, every system is built and operated by our own team.
